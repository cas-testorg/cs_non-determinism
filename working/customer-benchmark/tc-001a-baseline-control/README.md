Repeat the baseline nondeterminism discovery after resolving the environmental issue encountered in TC-001.

## Relationship to TC-001

TC-001 is retained as a pilot with limitations. TC-001A starts from a fresh Cursor conversation and sufficient disk space while keeping the discovery task and CTS scope unchanged.

Do not use TC-001 findings, TSan/Coverity ground truth, or prior candidate locations to steer this rerun.
